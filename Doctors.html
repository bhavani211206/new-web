Here’s a **15-line paragraph** summarizing the healthcare services provided by **King George Hospital** (commonly known as KGH) in Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh:
King George Hospital is one of the largest government hospitals in Visakhapatnam, offering comprehensive medical care to patients from Visakhapatnam district as well as neighbouring regions of Andhra Pradesh, Odisha, and Chhattisgarh.
Established nearly a century ago, the hospital operates 24×7 and manages a high patient load with over 1,000 beds, out-patient services, emergency care, and daily surgeries. It provides **general medicine, surgery, and emergency treatment** through its dedicated medical teams along with round-the-clock casualty and outpatient services.
