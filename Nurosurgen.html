A neurologist is a doctor who treats problems related to the brain, spinal cord, and nerves. The nervous system controls everything in our body, such as thinking, movement, memory, and feeling. If someone has frequent headaches, seizures, dizziness, memory loss, nerve pain, or difficulty in movement, they may need to consult a neurologist. Neurologists diagnose and treat conditions like migraines, epilepsy, stroke, paralysis, Parkinson’s disease, and other nerve disorders. They use different tests and medical knowledge to find the exact cause of the problem and provide proper treatment. A neurologist helps patients manage their symptoms, improve their quality of life, and maintain better brain and nerve health.
