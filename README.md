🌟 Project Overview

CityCare & King George Hospital Management System is a modern, multi-page healthcare web platform designed to simplify hospital information access, specialist consultation, and appointment booking.

Inspired by the real-world structure of King George Hospital and CityCare Hospital, this system provides a clean, user-friendly interface that connects patients with expert doctors and essential healthcare services — all in one place.

This platform ensures:

Easy navigation

Professional doctor profiles

Structured appointment booking

Clear service categorization

Emergency accessibility

📅 Appointment Page

📝 Book Your Consultation

Our streamlined booking system ensures a smooth and secure appointment process.

🔹 Simple 3-Step Booking Process

1️⃣ Fill in your personal and contact details
2️⃣ Select your preferred doctor and available date
3️⃣ Click “Book Now” to confirm

🎟 Instant Appointment Receipt

After submission, the system automatically generates a digital appointment receipt, which includes:

Patient name

Doctor selected

Appointment date & time

Unique reference ID


This confirmation guarantees your slot is reserved and provides a reference for your hospital visit.
