An ENT doctor, also known as an otolaryngologist, is a medical specialist who diagnoses and treats conditions related to the ear, nose, and throat. These doctors handle a wide range of health problems such as ear infections, hearing loss, sinus infections, tonsillitis, throat pain, voice disorders, nasal allergies, and breathing difficulties. The ear, nose, and throat are closely connected, so problems in one area can often affect the others, and an ENT doctor is specially trained to understand these connections. They also treat more serious conditions like tumors in the head and neck region, balance disorders, and chronic sinus problems. ENT doctors use advanced tools and tests to examine patients carefully and provide the right treatment, which may include medications, therapies, or surgical procedures when necessary. They also help patients with speech and swallowing difficulties and guide them on preventive care to maintain good ear, nose, and throat health. Consulting an ENT doctor ensures proper diagnosis and effective treatment, helping patients improve their overall quality of life and avoid long-term complications.
