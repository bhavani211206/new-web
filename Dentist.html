who treates dental problem.




A dentist is a medical professional who specializes in the care and treatment of teeth, gums, and overall oral health. Dentists help prevent, diagnose, and treat problems such as tooth decay, cavities, gum disease, bad breath, tooth sensitivity, and oral infections. Regular visits to a dentist are very important because they can detect early signs of dental problems and provide timely treatment before the condition becomes serious. Dentists perform procedures like cleaning teeth, filling cavities, extracting damaged teeth, placing crowns, performing root canal treatments, and aligning teeth with braces. They also educate patients about proper brushing and flossing techniques, healthy eating habits, and ways to maintain strong and healthy teeth. Good oral health is closely connected to overall body health, as infections in the mouth can sometimes affect other parts of the body. By visiting a dentist regularly and following proper dental care practices, individuals can maintain a bright smile, strong teeth, and better overall well-being.
