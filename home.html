King George Hospital

King George Hospital (KGH) is one of the oldest and largest government hospitals in Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh. Established in 1845 during British rule and named after King George V, the hospital plays a vital role in providing affordable healthcare to people from North Coastal Andhra Pradesh and nearby regions.
It is affiliated with Andhra Medical College, where medical students receive clinical training.
KGH offers multi-specialty services including emergency care, general medicine, surgery, maternity care, and intensive care facilities.
Serving thousands of patients every day, it is considered a lifeline for many poor and middle-class families.
We provide quality healthcare with modern facilities and expert doctors.
