Orthopedics:

Bone and joint care.




Orthopedics is a branch of medicine that focuses on the diagnosis, treatment, and prevention of disorders related to the bones, joints, muscles, ligaments, and tendons. An orthopedic doctor, also known as an orthopedist, treats conditions such as fractures, arthritis, back pain, joint pain, sports injuries, spine problems, and deformities of the bones. These specialists help patients who experience difficulty in movement, swelling in joints, muscle injuries, or bone-related accidents. Orthopedic doctors use physical examinations, X-rays, scans, and other tests to identify the exact problem and provide appropriate treatment. Treatment may include medications, physical therapy, lifestyle advice, supportive devices like braces, or surgical procedures such as joint replacement or fracture repair in severe cases. Orthopedics plays a very important role in helping people regain mobility, reduce pain, and improve their quality of life. With proper care and timely treatment from an orthopedic specialist, patients can recover faster and return to their daily activities with strength and confidence.
